# F-014 Selection System — Manual Test

Open `res://tests/scenes/party_member_smoke.tscn` in the 4.7.2 editor and press **F6**.
Three members spawn (slots: F1 **Detour**, F2 **Gap**, F3 **Rock**) and start walking; **Detour** starts selected.
Watch the overlay's `Selected:` row (top-right). The Output panel and console (`` ` ``) print `selection: …` on every change.
Pan with the **arrow keys** or press `V` to keep members on screen.

## Click
1. Left-click **Gap**: only Gap has the green ring and label. Overlay: `Selected: Gap`.
2. **Shift+click** Rock: Gap and Rock are both selected.
3. **Shift+click** Gap again: Gap is removed, Rock stays.
4. **Shift+click** Rock (the only one selected): nothing changes, because the last member can't be removed.
5. Left-click the floor, an obstacle, and the sky: the selection doesn't change and nothing prints.
6. Right-click a member that isn't selected: nothing changes (right-click isn't a selection input).

## Box
7. Left-drag across two members: a translucent green box follows the mouse. On release, exactly those two are selected
   (replacing the old selection). Try dragging in all four directions.
8. Select one member, then **Shift+drag** over another: both are selected.
9. Drag a box over empty floor: the selection doesn't change and nothing prints.
10. A tiny jiggle (under ~6 px) on a member still counts as a click (no box appears).
11. Start a drag, then release over the open console panel: the box disappears and the selection is applied.
12. Open the console and press inside its panel, then drag out: no box appears.

## Keys
13. **F1** / **F2** / **F3**: select only Detour / Gap / Rock. **F4–F6**: nothing happens (empty slots).
14. **Shift+F1**, **Shift+F3**: toggle those members in and out (never removing the last one).
15. **Ctrl+A** (**Cmd+A** on macOS) and **Home**: all three are selected. The camera must **not** pan left.
16. Open the console and press F2 or Home while its input has focus: the selection doesn't change.

## Debug
17. Console: `select 2` → Rock only; `select all`; `select none` → no rings, `Selected: —`; `pm_select 0` toggles Detour.
