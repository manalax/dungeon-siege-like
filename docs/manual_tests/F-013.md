# F-013 Mouse Picking — Manual Test

Open `res://tests/scenes/party_member_smoke.tscn` in the 4.7.2 editor and press **F6**.
Near the west spawn there's a small **gold box** (test pickup) and a thin **lever** (test interactable).
Press `V` / pan as needed to bring them on screen. Watch the overlay's `Hover:` row (top-right).

## Hover
1. Hover a party member: a **white outline** appears around its body (not its ring or nose) and its
   **name label** shows. Overlay: `Hover: actor <name>`. Move off: both disappear.
2. Hover a walking member: the highlight follows it even if you keep the mouse still while it walks under/away.
3. Hover the gold box: **gold** outline, `Hover: pickup TestPickup`.
4. Hover the lever: **light-blue** outline, `Hover: interactable TestLever`.
5. Hover the floor or an obstacle: no outline; overlay shows `ground (x, y, z)` updating as you move.
6. Hover the sky beyond the arena: `Hover: —`.

## Click
7. Left-click a member, the box, the lever, the floor; then right-click each. The Output panel (and the
   console, `` ` ``) prints e.g. `left click: actor Farmhand @ (...)`, `right click: ground Floor @ (...)`.
8. Mouse wheel and middle-drag still zoom/rotate the camera and print nothing.

## UI blocks picking
9. Open the console (`` ` ``) and click inside its panel: **no** click line is printed, and hovering over
   the panel clears any outline.
