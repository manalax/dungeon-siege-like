# F-012 RTS Camera — Manual Test

Open `res://tests/scenes/party_member_smoke.tscn` in the 4.7.2 editor and press **F6**.
The overlay (top-right) shows a `Camera:` row with mode, distance, yaw and pitch.

## Follow
1. The camera starts **following "Detour"** (member 0) as it walks around the wall. Movement is smooth:
   no stutter or shimmer, even on a high-refresh display. (Try `timescale 0.3` in the console too.)
2. Press **W** (or push the mouse to a screen edge): follow turns off (`Camera: free`) and the view pans.
3. Press **V**: the camera glides back to Detour and resumes following. Press **V** again: free.

## Inputs
4. **Mouse wheel**: zooms toward the screen centre; stops at the closest/furthest limit.
   Zoomed in the view is lower (~40°), zoomed out it looks more steeply down (~60°).
5. **Q / E**: rotates smoothly left/right around the pivot. **Middle-mouse drag**: rotates freely.
6. **WASD**: pans relative to the current view direction (W = "up the screen" whatever the rotation).
   Zoomed out, panning covers more ground per second.
7. Panning stops at the arena's edges (profile bounds).
8. Mouse at any screen edge pans that way; moving it back to the middle stops.

## Settings and focus
9. Open the console (`` ` ``) and type `wasd` into it — the camera must **not** move while typing.
10. Close the console. Settings are live; there is no settings UI until F-088, but you can confirm with
    a breakpoint/remote inspector on `Game.settings` (`camera_pan_speed`, `camera_rotate_speed`,
    `camera_zoom_speed`, `edge_scroll_enabled`).
11. Tactical pause stand-in: `timescale 0` — the characters freeze, but the camera still rotates, pans and zooms.
    Restore with `timescale 1`.
