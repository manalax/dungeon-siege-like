# F-010 Test Arena — Manual Test

1. In the 4.7.2 editor, enable **Debug → Visible Navigation** (menu bar).
2. Open `res://dungeon/test/test_arena.tscn` and press **F6** (run current scene).
3. Expect a green 40×40 m floor seen from above at ~50°, a long grey wall down the middle,
   three brown crates (east-south), a grey rock (west-south), and two pillars (east-north). Sky visible; obstacles cast shadows.
4. The navmesh overlay (blue) covers the floor with **holes around every obstacle**:
   - open passages at both ends of the wall,
   - a thin strip of navmesh through the gap between the two pillars,
   - **no** navmesh on top of the rock, crates, wall or pillars.
5. No errors or warnings in the Output panel.
