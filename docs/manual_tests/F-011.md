# F-011 Party Member Actor — Manual Test

## Smoke scene
1. In the 4.7.2 editor, open `res://tests/scenes/party_member_smoke.tscn` and press **F6**.
   (Optional: **Debug → Visible Navigation** to see the navmesh.)
2. Three blue capsules start on the west side, each with a green ring and a name label (all selected for the demo).
   Each has a small dark "nose" showing which way it faces.
3. Within ~10 s:
   - **Detour** walks around the end of the central wall to the east side and stops.
   - **Gap** walks straight between the two pillars and stops.
   - **Rock** walks toward the rock and stops beside it (its target was the rock's centre).
4. Check: starts/stops are quick but not instant, characters **turn smoothly** to face their heading,
   nobody clips into obstacles, and nobody jitters after stopping.
5. The Output panel prints `<name> arrived at (...)` three times and shows no errors.

## Console
6. Press `` ` `` and try:
   - `pm_move -15 15` → all three walk to the north-west corner.
   - `pm_move 15 -15 0` → only the first member walks to the south-east.
   - `pm_select 1` → toggles member 1's ring and label off/on.
   - `pm_move 0 0` → target is inside the wall; they stop at the nearest reachable spot beside it.
